# Sâm Ngọc Linh Product Catalog

Vocabulary for the public product catalog and editorial content managed by the site.

## Catalog

**Product**:
A distinct catalog item shown on the product listing and its own detail page. Different package sizes are represented as separate products.
_Avoid_: Product variant, when referring to a separately listed size

**Product category**:
A named grouping for the catalog. Each product has one primary product category, and a category page lists the products assigned to it.
_Avoid_: Product type

## Editorial content

**News article**:
A news item shown in the news list and opened on its own article page.
_Avoid_: Blog post

**Page section**:
A predesigned content block that an editor can add, remove, or reorder on a public page. Each page type allows only the section types designed for it.
_Avoid_: Frame
